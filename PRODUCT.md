# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Affluent travelers, private clients, and corporate customers evaluating a premium concierge and payment-card service in Singapore. They expect calm luxury, clear account choices, and fast paths to sign up or learn more.

## Product Purpose

CMS_Cakes will use a Cardaq Singapore-inspired visual system as its foundation: premium, pale, spacious, service-led, and conversion-oriented. Success means every page feels like part of the same concierge-grade brand system, with clear navigation, strong hero messaging, polished cards, and high-trust calls to action.

## Brand Personality

Premium, calm, precise. The interface should feel light, international, curated, and service-minded rather than loud or decorative.

## Anti-references

Do not use dark fintech dashboards, heavy gradients, saturated ecommerce styling, generic cake-shop pastels, cartoon bakery visuals, or rough handmade illustration. Avoid visual clutter, sharp aggressive contrast, and overcomplicated component chrome.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with trust: navigation, account actions, and pricing must feel structured and transparent.
2. Keep luxury quiet: use space, pale panels, soft borders, and disciplined typography instead of ornament.
3. Make actions obvious: primary sign-up actions use the pink pill button system; secondary actions use pink text links with arrow icons.
4. Build in modules: hero, sliders, cards, pricing, benefits, and service lists should reuse the same spacing and type rhythm.
5. Preserve lightness: white and near-white surfaces dominate, with navy text and pink accents doing the brand work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for all new text and controls. Respect reduced motion. Keep focus states visible, clickable targets at least 40px tall, and do not rely on pink alone to communicate state.
